What it shows

Six views of what your tools send.

Each one answers a question you have asked yourself about Ollama. Click a picture to see it full size.

LivePrompt X-rayRunsShared boxGPU mapCache Lab

What is each tool sending?

Live lists every turn as it arrives: which tool, which model, how much the cache kept.

When something is wrong, you get one plain sentence and the fix.

Your prompt, drawn to scale

Each turn is a bar. Pale parts were already in memory. Bright parts had to be read again.

Striped parts never reached the model.

One agent run, end to end

Every call on a timeline: loading the model, reading the prompt, writing the answer, waiting.

Steps that start from nothing are marked, so you see what your agent throws away.

Who cost whom?

One line per tool, sorted by the waiting time it caused the others.

Each line says what to change. A tool that cost nothing is listed too.

What fits on your card?

One bar the width of your graphics card: what is loaded, what is free.

Under it, every model you have pulled, with a plain verdict: fits now, fits alone, too big.

Measure the fix before you ship it

Paste your system prompt. Cache Lab finds what changes between turns and sends both orders to your own Ollama.

You get the measured difference, not an estimate.

Start it, point one tool at it, watch the first turn arrive.

$npx llmxray
