Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Our Commitment

LLMxRay is built with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data. The application runs entirely on your local machine.

2. No Data Collection

LLMxRay does not collect any user data. There are no analytics, no tracking pixels, no cookies, no telemetry, and no user accounts. Your conversations, benchmarks, embeddings, and training data never leave your machine.

3. Local Storage

All application data is stored locally in your browser’s IndexedDB and localStorage. This includes chat sessions, benchmark results, knowledge base documents, and training pairs. You have full control over this data and can delete it at any time through your browser settings.

4. Network Connections

LLMxRay only makes network requests to your local Ollama instance (typically localhost:11434). No external API calls are made. The contact form on this website submits to Google Forms, which is subject to Google’s privacy policy.

5. Contact Form

If you choose to use the contact form on this website, the information you provide (name, email, message) is submitted to Google Forms and stored in Google Sheets. This is the only data that leaves your machine, and it is provided voluntarily.

6. Third-Party Software

LLMxRay relies on Ollama for model inference. Ollama runs locally on your machine. We recommend reviewing Ollama’s privacy practices independently.

7. Open Source Transparency

LLMxRay is fully open source. You can audit every line of code to verify that no data is collected or transmitted. The source code is available on GitHub.

8. Children’s Privacy

LLMxRay does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since we collect no data at all, this is inherently satisfied.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please reach out via the Contact page or through our GitHub repository.
