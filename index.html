What goes wrong

Four things Ollama does without telling you.

Ollama forgot part of your chat

Your conversation outgrew the model's memory. Ollama dropped the oldest messages without a word, and the model made up what it no longer had.

If your instructions alone are too long, Ollama cuts their start instead.

Fix: raise num_ctx.

One timestamp, and the cache is gone

A value that changes near the top of your prompt makes the model re-read everything, every turn.

Move it to the end and the same turn costs almost nothing.

Fix: put dates, ids and counters at the end of the prompt.

Who is slowing Ollama down?

Two tools on one graphics card kept throwing out each other's model. Every call waited about 16 seconds for a reload.

Shared box names the tool, the cost to the others, and the setting to change.

Fix: keep_alive on the tool that unloads, num_ctx on the tool that asks for too much.

Your agent starts over at every step

An agent that opens a fresh conversation for each step re-reads its instructions every time.

Runs lays out every call, so you see which steps started from nothing and what each one cost.

Fix: keep one conversation across steps.
