The Lab · Diagnostics

Three questions about your model.

Where did it hesitate. Which setting was better. Is it confidently wrong. Each page answers one, on your machine.

Where did the model hesitate?

Every word is coloured by how sure the model was when it wrote it.

When Ollama does not report that, the page shows timing instead and says so.

Which setting was actually better?

The same prompt in up to four slots, side by side, with speed and length for each.

A word-level diff shows what changed between two answers.

Is it confidently wrong?

Benchmark suites scored on the model's own certainty, not on whether the answer looked right.

Wrong answers given with high certainty are the ones to worry about.
